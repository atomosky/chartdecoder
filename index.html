How to play Chart Decoder

  1. Start with Bootcamp. Learn the Greek and Latin building blocks (prefixes, roots, suffixes) plus the Rx shorthand used in orders.
  2. Pick a note. Each specialty has an inpatient, outpatient, or emergency note. Highlighted terms are the ones you'll decode.
  3. Decode, term by term. Choose the meaning. Right on the first try = 10 pts. After using a hint = 5 pts. Three in a row = streak bonus.
  4. Earn stars. 90%+ = 3 stars · 70%+ = 2 · 50%+ = 1. At the end, you'll see the whole note translated into plain language.

Why are there blanks in the notes?

Research and teaching datasets are de-identified before you ever see them. Under the HIPAA Safe Harbor method, 18 kinds of identifiers are removed, including names, dates (except year), MRNs, phone numbers, addresses, and ages over 89. Every dataset marks removed text differently:

  • ___ blanks (the style used in MIMIC-IV)
  • [**Name**] tags (the style used in MIMIC-III)
  • █████ black bars, or fake "surrogate" names in some datasets

Use the Redaction style menu above each note to practice reading all three. Tap any redaction to see what was removed and why.

Context is everything

The same letters can mean different things in different specialties. RA is room air in the ED and rheumatoid arthritis in rheumatology. AG is anion gap in the ICU and anticipatory guidance in pediatrics. Watch for these repeats.
